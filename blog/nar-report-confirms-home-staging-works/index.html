New NAR Report Confirms: Home Staging Works — and Here's the Proof
Fresh data shows staging delivers higher offers, faster sales, and happier buyers
By Tawni Oppenheim • August 12, 2025 • Market Insights

Why Staging Matters More Than Ever

If you've ever wondered whether home staging really makes a difference, the latest National Association of REALTORS® (NAR) data says it loud and clear: staging boosts both sale price and speed of sale.

In NAR's 2025 Profile of Home Staging, real estate professionals across the country reported measurable results from staging — and let's be honest, the numbers are worth paying attention to.

29%
of agents said staging led to a 1%–10% increase in the dollar value offered for their sellers' homes

Key Findings from the NAR Report

Higher Offers – That 29% number isn't just a statistic — it's money in your pocket. When you're talking about a $900,000 home in Oceanside, even a modest 1% bump equals $9,000. That's basically turning your staging bill into a money printer.

Faster Sales – Nearly half (49%) of sellers' agents reported staging reduced time on market, with 19% seeing a significant time reduction. Let's face it, every day your house sits on the market costs you money in carrying costs and stress.

83%
of buyers' agents said staging made it easier for buyers to picture the home as theirs

Most Important Rooms to Stage – Here's the kicker: the data shows exactly where to focus your staging dollars for maximum impact:

  • Living room (37%) — This is your money room
  • Primary bedroom (34%) — Where buyers envision their daily retreat
  • Kitchen (23%) — The heart of the home
"After seeing the NAR data, I started recommending staging to all my sellers. The difference in buyer engagement is night and day — more showings, more offers, higher prices."
— Jennifer M., Realtor®, Carlsbad

Why This Matters for Oceanside & North County San Diego Sellers

Our market is competitive, with buyers comparing multiple listings daily. The difference between a "nice" home and a must-have home often comes down to presentation.

Buyers in Oceanside, Carlsbad, Vista, Encinitas, and surrounding areas expect homes to look as polished as the ones they see on HGTV. Here's the reality check: NAR's survey found that 48% of buyers expect staged homes to match TV-show quality — and 58% are disappointed when they don't.
