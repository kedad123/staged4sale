The Power of Home Staging: Data-Backed Benefits for Sellers

Discover how professional home staging can significantly impact your property's sale, leading to faster sales, higher prices, and increased buyer interest. Here are some compelling statistics from leading real estate authorities:

Accelerated Sales Velocity

  • Staged homes sell up to 3 times faster than non-staged homes. Source: Real Estate Staging Association (RESA) | Kimberlee Real Estate
  • Average houses that are staged sell 5–11 times faster than those that are not staged. Source: International Association of Home Staging Professionals (IAHSP) | Raleigh Realty
  • The average Days on Market (DOM) for a median-priced home is 83 days before staging, which drops to an average of just 7 days after staging. Source: IAHSP | Raleigh Realty
  • 90% of homes staged before going on the market sell in 90% less time. Source: RESA | Raleigh Realty
  • Staged homes typically spend between 33% and 50% less time on the market than non-staged properties. Source: HomeAdvisor | Bankrate

Maximized Financial Returns

  • Staged homes can sell for up to 15% more than non-staged homes. Source: RESA | Kimberlee Real Estate
  • Staged homes sold for nearly $70,000 over their list price, on average (2024 data). Source: RESA | Bankrate
  • Staged homes sold for 25% more than homes without staging. Source: Realtor Magazine | Raleigh Realty
  • Over 85% of staged homes sell for their asking price or above. Source: RESA | Kimberlee Real Estate
  • The average ROI for staged vacant homes is an impressive 586%, with all sellers seeing an average return of 5-15%. Source: Bankrate | Raleigh Realty
  • An average staging investment of just 1.3% of the home's value has been shown to yield a 7.1% over-list ROI. Source: RESA | Jeff Marples Real Estate

Enhanced Buyer Visualization & Engagement

  • 82% to 83% of buyer's agents reported that staging made it easier for their clients to visualize the property as their future home. Source: National Association of Realtors (NAR) | Kimberlee Real Estate, PA Realtors, Raleigh Realty
  • 40% to 41% of buyers are more willing to tour a home in person after viewing professionally staged photos online. Source: NAR | Kimberlee Real Estate, Jeff Marples Real Estate
  • Buyers tend to linger in staged homes for an average of 40 minutes, compared to just six minutes in a vacant home. Source: NAR | Raleigh Realty
  • 27% of buyers were more willing to overlook other property faults when viewing a staged home. Source: NAR | Raleigh Realty
