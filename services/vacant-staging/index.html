Vacant Home Staging

Transform empty properties into irresistible buyer magnets with complete professional furnishing

The Power of Vacant Home Staging

Empty homes struggle to sell. Buyers can't envision the space's potential, rooms feel cold and uninviting, and properties linger on the market. Professional staging changes everything.

Vacant Home Staging Results

83% of buyers' agents say staging makes it easier for buyers to visualize the property (NAR 2023)
18% increase in dollar value offered by buyers for staged homes vs. non-staged (RESA 2023)
73% of buyers' agents say staging decreases time on market (NAR 2023)

Our vacant home staging service provides complete transformation using carefully curated furniture, artwork, and accessories. We create warm, inviting spaces that help buyers emotionally connect with your property from the moment they walk through the door.

Unlike basic furniture rental companies, we're ASPM™ certified staging professionals who understand buyer psychology, current design trends, and local market expectations. Every piece is strategically chosen and placed to highlight your home's best features while minimizing any drawbacks.

Our Vacant Staging Process

1

Property Assessment & Design Plan

We evaluate your vacant property's layout, architectural features, natural light, and target buyer demographic to create a customized staging design plan.

2

Furniture Selection & Procurement

Our team selects appropriate furniture, artwork, and accessories from our extensive inventory, ensuring pieces complement your home's style and scale.

3

Professional Installation

Our staging team delivers and installs all furnishings, creating picture-perfect rooms that showcase your home's full potential and lifestyle appeal.

4

Finishing Touches & Final Styling

We add final styling elements, fresh flowers, and ensure every detail is perfect for photography and showings. Your staged home will be move-in ready and photo-perfect.
