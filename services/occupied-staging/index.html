Occupied Home Staging: Your Home, Market-Ready

Staging your home while living in it requires skill, creativity, and understanding of daily life needs. We make it work beautifully for both you and potential buyers.

Occupied home staging is the art of transforming a lived-in space into a market-ready showcase while keeping it functional for daily living. Our ASPM™ certified approach combines your existing furniture and belongings with strategic rental pieces, expert rearrangement, and professional styling to create spaces that appeal to buyers without disrupting your life.

We understand that selling your home while living in it presents unique challenges. Our process is designed to minimize disruption while maximizing your home's sales appeal, working around your schedule and lifestyle needs throughout the staging period.

Why Choose Occupied Staging?

Cost Effective

Typically 30-50% less expensive than vacant staging since we work with your existing furniture and belongings.

Stay Comfortable

Continue living in your home throughout the staging process with minimal lifestyle disruption.

Quick Implementation

Most occupied stagings completed in 1-2 days, much faster than full vacant staging installation.

Use What You Own

Maximize the potential of your existing furniture and décor with professional rearrangement and additions.

How to Prepare for Occupied Staging

Here's what you can do before we arrive to ensure a smooth staging process

Declutter & Depersonalize

  • Remove family photos and personal memorabilia
  • Pack away collections and personal items
  • Clear countertops and surfaces
  • Minimize children's toys and school items
  • Store seasonal clothing and linens

Deep Clean Throughout

  • Complete thorough cleaning of all rooms
  • Address any needed repairs or touch-ups
  • Clean windows for maximum natural light
  • Ensure all surfaces are spotless
  • Remove pet odors and evidence

Create Storage Solutions

  • Organize closets and storage areas
  • Consider temporary storage unit if needed
  • Use garage or basement for excess items
  • Create designated spaces for daily necessities
  • Set up "staging maintenance" supplies

Coordinate With Family

  • Discuss staging goals with all family members
  • Establish showing-day routines
  • Plan for pet care during showings
  • Create systems for daily maintenance
  • Set realistic expectations for living staged
