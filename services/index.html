Vacant Home Staging

Complete furnishing and styling of empty homes to help buyers envision the space's full potential and create emotional connections.

Empty homes struggle to sell. Our vacant staging service provides complete transformation using carefully curated furniture, artwork, and accessories. We create warm, inviting spaces that help buyers emotionally connect with your property from the moment they walk through the door.

Unlike basic furniture rental companies, we're ASPM™ certified staging professionals who understand buyer psychology, current design trends, and local market expectations. Every piece is strategically chosen and placed to highlight your home's best features.

$2,500 - $6,500+

Varies by home size • Monthly rental • Professional installation

Occupied Home Staging

Transform your lived-in space using a combination of your existing furniture and our professional pieces to create a market-ready home.

Occupied home staging is the art of transforming a lived-in space into a market-ready showcase while keeping it functional for daily living. Our approach combines your existing furniture with strategic rental pieces, expert rearrangement, and professional styling.

We understand that selling your home while living in it presents unique challenges. Our process minimizes disruption while maximizing your home's sales appeal, working around your schedule and lifestyle needs throughout the staging period.

$1,500 - $4,500+

Varies by scope • Mix of existing & rental items • Flexible solutions

Best for: Sellers still living in their home who have good-quality furniture and want to enhance their space without major disruption.

Staged4Home is where staging expertise meets interior design for homeowners who aren't selling – you're staying, and you deserve to live beautifully. We apply our professional staging expertise to create spaces designed for your daily enjoyment, comfort, and long-term happiness.
