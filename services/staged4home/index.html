Welcome to Staged4Home

Where staging expertise meets interior design for homeowners who love where they live

Staged4Home is our signature service for homeowners who aren't selling – you're staying, and you deserve to live beautifully. We apply our professional staging expertise to create spaces designed for your daily enjoyment, comfort, and long-term happiness.

Unlike traditional interior design that focuses on personal taste alone, or staging that focuses solely on selling, Staged4Home combines both approaches. We create spaces that reflect your personality while incorporating proven design principles that enhance daily living, functionality, and long-term satisfaction.

Our ASPM™ certified approach means we understand what makes spaces feel welcoming, comfortable, and visually appealing – principles we now apply to create your perfect living environment rather than impress potential buyers.

Our Design Philosophy

Function Meets Beauty

Every design choice serves both aesthetic appeal and daily functionality. Beautiful spaces should work beautifully for your life.

Timeless Appeal

We create designs that feel fresh now and remain appealing for years to come, avoiding trendy elements that quickly become dated.

Personal Expression

Your home should reflect your personality and lifestyle while maintaining sophisticated design principles that enhance daily living.

Sustainable Choices

We focus on quality pieces that last, repurposing existing items when possible, and making thoughtful investments in your home's future.

The Staged4Home Process

1

Lifestyle Discovery

We begin with an in-depth consultation about how you live, work, entertain, and relax in your space. Understanding your daily routines drives every design decision.

2

Space Assessment & Vision

We evaluate your current space, existing furniture, architectural features, and potential. Together, we develop a vision that maximizes your home's possibilities.

3

Design Development

We create detailed design plans including furniture layouts, color palettes, lighting design, and décor concepts tailored to your lifestyle and preferences.

4

Sourcing & Procurement

We handle all furniture and décor sourcing, working within your budget to find pieces that deliver both beauty and functionality for your daily needs.

5

Installation & Styling

Our team handles delivery, installation, and complete styling, transforming your space with minimal disruption to your daily routine.

6

Final Touches & Living

We add the finishing touches that make the space uniquely yours, then teach you how to maintain and evolve your newly designed space.
